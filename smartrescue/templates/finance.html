How it is calculated

Revenue = actual price x units for completed sales only. COGS = purchase cost x units sold. Gross = revenue - COGS. Net = gross - recorded expenses. Loss = cost of units left on expired listings. Discount = (original - actual price) x units sold. Donation value = original price x units in completed donations, kept separate. Potential figures are estimates from available stock and are never included in revenue or realised profit.
